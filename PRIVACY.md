## Summary

ShotSync copies screenshots and screen recordings from your Android phone
**directly to your own Mac**. There is no ShotSync server or cloud. We do not
collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data, and the app contains
no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

## What the Android app accesses and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots and screen recordings (photos/videos permission) | The app's only function: detect new screenshots/recordings and copy them | Sent only to the Mac you paired, over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3, certificate pinned to your Mac) |
| File names, sizes, SHA-256 checksums | De-duplication and integrity checking | Sent with each file to your Mac |
| Camera | Scan the pairing QR code shown by your Mac. Frames are processed on the device and never stored or sent | Nowhere |
| A device name you choose and a random device ID generated by the app | Lets your Mac show and revoke this phone. No hardware model, IMEI, advertising ID or account is used | Your Mac only |
| Network state / Wi-Fi state | Upload only when your Mac is reachable; optional Wi-Fi-only mode | Stays on the phone |
| Notifications | Ongoing "monitoring" indicator and transfer notices | Stays on the phone |

A pairing token for your Mac is stored on the phone encrypted with the
Android Keystore. App data is excluded from Google cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer.

Diagnostic logs are kept on the phone (they never include file contents or
tokens). They leave the phone only if **you** choose "Export diagnostics" and
share the file yourself.

If you configure a Tailscale host name, traffic to your Mac travels over your
own Tailscale network; ShotSync does not contact Tailscale or any third
party itself. mDNS/Bonjour is used on your local network to find your Mac.

## The Mac receiver

The receiver runs on your Mac and writes received files to the folder you
choose. It stores its configuration, the list of paired phones, their access
tokens (in the macOS Keychain) and local logs in
`~/Library/Application Support/ShotSync`. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Retention and deletion

- Files on your Mac are yours; delete them like any other file.
- On the phone, the upload queue only references your existing media; we
  never delete or modify your photos or videos.
- Unpair from either side to revoke access. Uninstalling the app deletes all
  of its data on the phone; `shotsync uninstall` removes the receiver and
  (unless `--keep-data` is used) its data on the Mac.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or
delete on request, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

## Children

ShotSync is a general utility not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights (GDPR)

We process no personal data on our own systems. You keep all rights under
the GDPR, including to contact the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD,
www.aepd.es).
